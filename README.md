### Midiboard
<sub>THE NEED: A CONTROLLER THAT FITS MY HANDS ON STAGE</sub> · **[On the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6801598625)** · iPhone & iPad

<a href="https://midiboard.bulatapps.com/"><img src="https://bulatapps.com/img/splash-midiboard.jpg" width="480" align="right" alt="Midiboard on a phone held in landscape: a step sequencer, footswitches, a pad bank, chord buttons, a bend fader and a balance knob."></a>

I was already using my phone as a MIDI controller on stage, but where the buttons went was always
somebody else's call. In Midiboard the screen is an empty grid: pads, keys, chords, faders, XY, step
sequencers, whatever you need, wherever your hand goes.

It runs on iPhone and iPad and sends standard MIDI over Bluetooth or Wi-Fi: Ableton, Logic, Gig
Performer, hardware synths, whatever you play.

**[midiboard.bulatapps.com →](https://midiboard.bulatapps.com/)**

### Crossleaf
<sub>THE NEED: ONE SEARCH ACROSS EVERY MANUAL</sub> · **[On the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6819022919?ct=github)** · iPhone & iPad

<a href="https://crossleaf.bulatapps.com/"><img src="https://bulatapps.com/img/splash-crossleaf.jpg" width="480" align="right" alt="Crossleaf on iPad: a search for pressure across four handbooks, results grouped by document, the matching page open with the word highlighted."></a>

It started with flight crews searching a term through hundreds of pages of manuals, one file at a
time. Students and doctors turned out to have the same problem. Add all your PDFs, search once, and
see every document and page it appears on in one list. Scanned pages work too.

It never connects to the internet, because the documents people search most are usually the ones
that shouldn't leave the device. Free to download; one payment for unlimited documents, no
subscription.

**[crossleaf.bulatapps.com →](https://crossleaf.bulatapps.com/)**

### Kırışalım
<sub>THE NEED: THE MONTH-END SUM BETWEEN ROOMMATES</sub> · **[On the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6816543150)** · iPhone · Android in testing · [web](https://web.kirisalim.bulatapps.com/)

<a href="https://kirisalim.bulatapps.com/"><img src="https://bulatapps.com/img/splash-kirisalim.jpg" width="480" align="right" alt="Kırışalım on two phones: the house chat, and the month-end card showing each share and who pays whom."></a>

Every month, the same sum and the same argument with your roommates. Rent, bills, groceries: put
them in Kırışalım and at the end of the month it works out who owes whom, in as few transfers as
possible.

It never touches the money: you pay each other, whoever receives it taps "got it". It's the one app
of mine with accounts and a server, because a house's ledger has to look the same on everyone's
phone. The name is Turkish for "let's split it".

**[kirisalim.bulatapps.com →](https://kirisalim.bulatapps.com/)**

### Little, but useful
<sub>HOW I WORK</sub>

When an app starts from a need, there's no room for extras. Even the Mac apps' sounds are generated
in code, and these sites are plain HTML.

None of them collect anything: no analytics, no ads, no tracking. Apart from Kırışalım, they don't
connect to the internet at all.

I play guitar and keys and make music; that's where most of my needs come from.

Before apps I worked on embedded systems and .NET, which is where the habit of counting bytes
came from.

<sub>Swift · AppKit · Core Animation · AVAudioEngine · UIKit · CoreBluetooth · Flutter · Dart · Supabase · TypeScript · C / C++ · Python</sub>

### Sharing the parts that are useful
<sub>OPEN SOURCE</sub>

When something I wrote for one app looks useful elsewhere, I pull it out and open-source it.

**[QuietOverlay](https://github.com/safabulat/quiet-overlay)** · *Swift package · macOS 13+ · MIT*<br>
The shell underneath the desk toys. A whole-screen overlay window that clicks fall straight
through — except where you say they should not — and that can never steal keyboard focus
from the app you were typing in.

Free, MIT licensed.
